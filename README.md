# Vocal Warm-ups

A free Mac app for vocal warm-ups.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/corylamb/vocal-warmups/releases/latest)** — click `VocalWarmups.zip` under "Assets".

Requires macOS 15 or later (Intel or Apple Silicon).

## Install

1. Double-click `VocalWarmups.zip` to unzip it.
2. Drag **Vocal Warm-ups** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Double-click it. macOS will say it can't verify the app — click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Vocal Warm-ups. Enter your password.
5. It opens normally from now on.

Your settings and students are saved on your own Mac only.
